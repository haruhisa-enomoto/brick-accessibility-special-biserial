# Paper correspondence

One row per numbered statement of the [manuscript](manuscript/README.md)
*Brick accessibility for brick-finite special biserial algebras* (revision of
October 18, 2026), in source order. Numbers come from a two-pass `pdflatex`
build of the snapshot; `main.tex:N` gives the source line. Lean names are in the
namespace `BrickAccessibility`, and file paths are relative to
[`BrickAccessibility/`](../BrickAccessibility).

Status words: `defined`; `covered`; `covered (different route)`; `expository`.
No `sorry` remains, and every input used by the proofs is proved in this
repository, including the cited results of Butler–Ringel, Wald–Waschbüsch,
Plamondon and Skowroński–Waschbüsch. Out of scope: the genericity sentences of
Remark 2.2, the examples and the questions. The quotient closure cited in
Remark 5.7 is proved in the path-data form that the argument uses.

The local `Ext1` is the derivation quotient; there is no formal comparison with
categorical or Yoneda Ext. This is an interface boundary, not an extra axiom in the
endpoint proofs. The Skowroński–Waschbüsch step returns path data together with
the independence of vertices and arrows modulo paths of length two, from which
`PathData.isSpecialBiserial_of_pathData` builds `Statement.IsSpecialBiserial`.
